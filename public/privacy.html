Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

FitForge ("we", "the app") is a workout-planning web app available at fitforge-app-rygc.onrender.com. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and display name. If you sign up with email, we store your password only as a secure one-way hash. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your workout data: the workout plans you create (exercises, sets, reps, rest times, weights) and your completed workout sessions (date, duration, sets completed).
  • Shared plans (optional): if you choose to share a workout plan, its name, category and exercises are shown to all FitForge users together with your display name. We also count how many users viewed and added each shared plan. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • Usage information: basic activity events such as sign-ins, app opens, and when a workout is started, finished or ended early, with timestamps. We use these to show you your statistics and to understand how the app is used overall.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log standard request information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To save your plans and workouts and sync them across your devices.
  • To show workout comparisons (previous time, weekly and monthly averages).
  • To display plans you choose to share on the Shared plans page, along with view and add counts.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the app, including aggregate usage statistics viewed by the app administrator.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

When you choose "Continue with Google", FitForge requests only the basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile). This data is used solely to sign you in and identify your account. It is not shared with third parties or used for any other purpose. FitForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in a database hosted by Render (render.com) in the EU (Frankfurt). The app administrator can see account details and usage statistics (such as number of plans and workouts) in order to run the service. We share data with service providers only as needed to run the app (hosting, and email delivery if enabled), or if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app (tap your initial on the home screen → Delete my account), or by emailing us. Deletion is permanent.

Security

Connections are encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to the database is restricted. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children

FitForge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
